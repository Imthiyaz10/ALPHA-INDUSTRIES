ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Specialists in Vermiculite Exfoliation

Alpha Industries focuses on the industrial exfoliation of vermiculite, a naturally occurring mineral. Through controlled thermal processing, natural vermiculite expands into lightweight, accordion-like particles.

What is Vermiculite Exfoliation?

Vermiculite contains water molecules between its mineral layers. When exposed to rapid, controlled heating, this water turns into vapour and causes the mineral layers to expand. This process is called thermal exfoliation.

01. Raw Vermiculite

Natural vermiculite is selected and prepared for processing.

02. Controlled Heating

The mineral is exposed to rapid, controlled heating.

03. Thermal Expansion

Water between the mineral layers vaporizes, causing expansion.

04. Expanded Vermiculite

The resulting material is lightweight and has an expanded, accordion-like structure.
