ABOUT OUR COMPANY

Understanding Vermiculite Exfoliation

Vermiculite is a naturally occurring mineral belonging to the phyllosilicate group. It has a layered structure and contains water between its mineral layers.

When vermiculite is exposed to rapid, controlled heating, the trapped water turns into steam. This causes the mineral layers to expand, creating a lightweight, accordion-like structure.

What is Exfoliation?

Industrial exfoliation is the thermal expansion of natural vermiculite. It transforms compact mineral particles into expanded, lightweight material.
